## Goal

Make text preservation and deliberate transformation explicit invariants.

## Approved focus

- authoritative source encoding with detection confidence and explicit override;
- UTF-8, Windows-1252, UTF-16 LE/BE, and UTF-32 LE/BE;
- BOM recognition, preservation, and control;
- LF, CRLF, CR, and mixed-EOL detection plus deliberate migration;
- explicit encoding conversion;
- malformed-byte policy and untouched-byte preservation;
- save round-trip verification; and
- dirty/save-point semantics around transformations.

## Canonical acceptance rule

Opening and saving a file must not silently change its encoding or line endings.
