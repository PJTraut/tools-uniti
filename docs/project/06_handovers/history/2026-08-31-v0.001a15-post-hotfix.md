## Mac/PySide6 verification

Two Qt Find/Replace tests initially exposed a completion race. Fast background searches could finish before a 40 ms polling timer applied their results, while `busy` already reported false. Callers could therefore observe zero results after apparent completion.

Commit `6b81185` replaced timer polling with a Future completion callback that emits a queued Qt signal. The panel remains busy until the GUI thread applies the payload, avoiding both premature observation and re-entrant inline completion.
