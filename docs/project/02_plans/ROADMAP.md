## Queue rules

- A milestone enters this roadmap only after purpose, scope, architectural direction, and acceptance criteria are approved.
- Normally exactly one milestone is active; later approved milestones are queued in explicit order.
- Queued milestones are implemented in listed order unless an explicit roadmap decision changes the sequence.
- Parked capabilities are not implicit roadmap items and carry no version or delivery promise.
- After verification, remove the milestone from this queue, move its records to `03_implemented`, update current truth, and refresh the handover.
