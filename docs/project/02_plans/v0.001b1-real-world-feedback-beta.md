## Goal

Promote a completely green A24 candidate to B1 and stabilize the private executable product through bounded, opt-in, cross-platform feedback from independent real-world users.

## Admission gate

- every required A24 acceptance item is `PASS` on one exact candidate;
- local, hosted, clean-host, bundle, health, startup, recovery, repair, Safe Session, performance, reproducibility, and inventory evidence names that candidate;
- A24 is preserved as implemented history before a separate B1 promotion commit; and
- display/package identity advances to `v0.001b1` / `0.1b1` without creating a tag, release, public package, installer publication, or updater.

`WARN`, `FAIL`, `INVALID`, `NOT RUN`, a required skip, or mismatched candidate evidence blocks admission.

## Approved focus

- preserve a feature freeze while correcting defects found through independent use;
- qualify same-commit macOS, Windows, and Linux executable bundles before feedback opens;
- include exactly one visible top-level UTF-8 `BETA_TESTER_INFO.txt` in every distribution, capped at 64 KiB and verified against the bundle manifest;
- accept only opt-in, manually submitted feedback and optional user-reviewed sanitized A23 support bundles;
- retain only bounded content-free feedback classification and verification evidence in Git;
- require deterministic reproduction and regression coverage for every accepted defect; and
- pause broader distribution for suspected data loss, corruption, unsafe overwrite/repair, recovery-evidence destruction, or a supported-platform startup dead end.

## Required feedback gate

- at least fourteen calendar days on the current qualifying behavior candidate;
- at least three independent human testers, with macOS, Windows, and Linux each represented;
- ordinary open/edit/save/explicit-Quit use by every tester;
- collective coverage of large files, encoding/EOL and malformed input, regex replacement, recovery/Safe Session, repair, and multi-window/split/docking behavior;
- every report triaged and every accepted correction regression-tested and verified on applicable platforms;
- complete final-candidate source, executable, clean-host, health, recovery, bundle, privacy, and performance gates; and
- zero unresolved critical safety findings.

Synthetic smoke, CI, benchmark activity, developer self-use, duplicate tester identities, and unverifiable builds do not satisfy the human feedback gate.

## Privacy and distribution boundary

UNITI performs no automatic telemetry, diagnostic upload, feedback submission, account creation, or background networking. Raw reports, support archives, screenshots, reproduction documents, paths, document/search/replacement content, personal data, and machine identifiers remain outside Git and CI artifacts.

Qualified builds are supplied manually to named private testers. Promotion and feedback completion do not infer a Git tag, GitHub release, public package, next version, or general-public distribution.
