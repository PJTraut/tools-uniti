Current source identity: `v0.001b3` / `0.1b3`. All reviewed BF-012–BF-068 source code integrated on `main` is in scope below (plus BF-040/042, reported earlier but implemented after this range — see "Workstreams added after initial scope"); items explicitly deferred, escalated, or out of scope are called out individually rather than implied by the range. The user-directed promotion and separation of source identity from release qualification are recorded in [ADR-0008](../05_decisions/ADR-0008-b3-version-and-qualification.md), continuing [ADR-0007](../05_decisions/ADR-0007-beta-source-version-and-qualification.md)'s B1→B2 pattern.

## Goal

Validate and refine the integrated beta source produced since B2 — dominated by the P1 Find/Replace job/result-model refactor and its dependents, file-type syntax highlighting, and a menu-bar restructuring — close the remaining feedback checks, and retain evidence-based qualification for executable delivery.

## Release qualification boundary

B3 source development is active. Qualified executable delivery still requires the outstanding A22 real-use days, A23 executable health/recovery delivery, A24 stabilization, and B1 fourteen-day independent executable feedback with three testers covering macOS, Windows, and Linux. These gates remain open; source version advancement does not complete their milestones. B2's own outstanding native-host and hosted-CI confirmations (BF-001–BF-010) also remain open and are unaffected by this promotion.

Physical Chinese/Korean IME qualification, affected-host BF-002 confirmation, and same-commit hosted validation remain pending. See [Current Status](../01_current/STATUS.md) for evidence and [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for the qualification order.

## Workstreams added after initial scope

Per the project grammar, a milestone may gain workstreams while still active; each moves to [Implemented](../03_implemented/README.md) individually as it lands, without requiring the whole milestone to close first.

- **RTL/bidi content support (Arabic + Hebrew)** — [BF-064](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md#bf-064--rtlbidi-content-support-arabic--hebrew), planned in [2026-09-15-rtl-bidi-support-plan.md](2026-09-15-rtl-bidi-support-plan.md). Un-parks BF-043's escalated architecture finding. Scoped as content-level bidi only (application chrome stays left-to-right). Content-level correctness is implemented and tested: direction detection, shaping/layout math, arrow-key/word-movement caret semantics (paragraph-level, not per-run embedding-level — a named, documented limitation), a highlight-rect fix for selection/match/invalid-byte painting, whitespace-marker positioning, and — the most significant finding, 2026-09-16 — a caret-freeze bug where typing or IME-composing into a right-to-left line never visually advanced the caret, affecting all RTL typing. **Remaining, explicitly deferred:** full multi-rect selection splitting at an embedded-run boundary, the deeper non-wrapped multi-checkpoint scrolling case, and native IME/physical qualification (no native hardware available).
- **Bundled font selection policy** — [BF-066](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md#bf-066--bundled-font-selection-policy-and-a-fully-sourced-arabic--hebrew-plan), a durable policy in [ADR-0010](../05_decisions/ADR-0010-bundled-font-selection-policy.md) plus an executed [Arabic + Hebrew bundling plan](../03_implemented/milestones/2026-09-16-arabic-hebrew-font-bundling-plan.md) (Noto Naskh Arabic and Noto Sans Hebrew, both hash-verified). Implemented 2026-09-16: 15 faces now registered (up from 13), closing BF-064's outstanding font gap; the registration code derives its expected face count from the manifest instead of a hardcoded literal (ADR-0010's registration-code contract). Full suite green (1,888 passed, 6 platform skips).
- **Global editor font weight, with hotkeys** — [BF-067](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md#bf-067--global-editor-font-weight-with-hotkeys). Implemented 2026-09-16, architected as a direct extension of the existing zoom feature (per-view state with a persisted `Settings`/`ViewRecord` default, not a new live cross-window broadcast mechanism): `Ctrl+Shift+=`/`Ctrl+Shift+-`/`Ctrl+Shift+0` step through Qt's nine named weights. Required a `SESSION_SCHEMA` bump to 5 for per-view persistence. Full suite green (1,900 passed, 6 platform skips).
- **Per-document task-pool fairness and session-schema extensibility** — [BF-040](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md#bf-040-p2--per-document-task-pool-fairness-and-multi-document-benchmark-foundation) and [BF-042](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md#bf-042-p4--session-schema-is-being-bumped-one-field-at-a-time), the two remaining escalated architecture findings from the P1 Find/Replace rework review, planned together in [2026-09-17-pool-1-task-fairness-and-schema-flexibility-plan.md](../03_implemented/milestones/2026-09-17-pool-1-task-fairness-and-schema-flexibility-plan.md). Implemented 2026-09-17: BF-040's `PriorityWorkerPool` now round-robins across documents within each priority tier instead of pure FIFO, so a burst of same-priority tasks from one document can no longer starve another document's task at the same tier — the foundation BF-030/031 (Pool 1 of the [outstanding work pools](2026-09-16-outstanding-work-pools.md)) need before their benchmarks mean anything. BF-042 bumped `SESSION_SCHEMA` to 6 (the deliberate last hard bump this mechanism needs) and added an open-ended, forward-compatible `extra` mapping to `ViewRecord`, `DocumentRecord`, and `FindReplaceManifestRecord`, so future optional per-document/per-panel state no longer requires a hard schema bump and full migration. Full suite green (1,960 passed, 6 platform skips).
