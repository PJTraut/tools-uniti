# UNITI v0.001b4 — Compare, Character Inspector & Per-View Settings Beta

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: active
Roadmap order: 1
Feedback: [Beta Feedback Log](../BETA_FEEDBACK.md)

Current source identity: `v0.001b4` / `0.1b4`. All reviewed BF-019, BF-070, BF-072, BF-073, and BF-075 source code integrated on `main` since B3 is in scope below; BF-071 (multiline-aware syntax highlighting) is already recorded as a B3 workstream and is not repeated here. Items explicitly deferred, escalated, or out of scope are called out individually rather than implied by the range. The user-directed promotion and separation of source identity from release qualification are recorded in [ADR-0012](../05_decisions/ADR-0012-b4-version-and-qualification.md), continuing [ADR-0008](../05_decisions/ADR-0008-b3-version-and-qualification.md)'s B2→B3 pattern.

## Goal

Validate and refine the integrated beta source produced since B3 — dominated by Compare's rework onto real editor views with full parity (whitespace, syntax, theme, zoom, cross-pane highlighting, intra-line diff), the Character Inspector's list+detail redesign, and per-view settings replacing single-global whitespace/tab-width/theme/syntax-profile behavior — plus a shared toggle-window state-persistence mechanism both Compare and Character Inspector now use, close the remaining feedback checks, and retain evidence-based qualification for executable delivery.

## Release qualification boundary

B4 source development is active. Qualified executable delivery still requires the outstanding A22 real-use days, A23 executable health/recovery delivery, A24 stabilization, and B1 fourteen-day independent executable feedback with three testers covering macOS, Windows, and Linux. These gates remain open; source version advancement does not complete their milestones. B2's and B3's own outstanding native-host and hosted-CI confirmations also remain open and are unaffected by this promotion.

Physical Chinese/Korean IME qualification, affected-host BF-002 confirmation, and same-commit hosted validation remain pending. BF-019's fix in this batch has not yet been re-confirmed natively beyond the user's own local report. See [Current Status](../01_current/STATUS.md) for evidence and [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for the qualification order.
